📁 How students add their own profile page

  1. Copy students/pages/template.html and rename it to your own id, e.g. priya-singh.html.
  2. Fill in your details in that file (bio, programming skills, reasoning skills, hobbies, area you'd like to explore, contact).
  3. Add your photo to students/photos/ (optional — skip this to show an initials circle instead).
  4. Add one entry for yourself in students/students-data.js and set hasPage: true.

Your card then appears automatically below and links straight to your page. Full step-by-step instructions are also written at the top of students-data.js.
