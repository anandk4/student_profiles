Put student photo files in this folder.

Name each photo to match the student's page "slug" in students-data.js,
for example:

  student-01.jpg   ->  matches slug "student-01"
  priya-singh.jpg  ->  matches slug "priya-singh"

Then set that student's "photo" field in ../students-data.js to:

  students/photos/<filename>

Square photos, at least 300x300px, look best. JPG or PNG both work.
If a student has no photo, leave their "photo" field as "" and the site
will automatically show a colored initials circle instead.

You can delete this file once photos have been added.
