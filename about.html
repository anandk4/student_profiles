Who We Are

Skill-first AI education

The Department of Artificial Intelligence was established under the School of Skill Sciences to prepare students for real careers in AI, machine learning and data engineering. Our curriculum pairs strong mathematical and computing fundamentals with applied labs, live projects and industry mentorship.
